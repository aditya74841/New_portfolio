I have a problem.

So many things come into my mind. Some I solve and save in notes. Some get researched. And very little gets into planning. Very little gets into execution. But none of them get completed.

This is not just a blogging problem. This is a general issue I face in my life.

## The Two Things I Was Missing

When I realized this pattern, I found two missing pieces:
1. **Planning**
2. **Deadlines**

And during that time, I learned every concept — but not in a structured format.

Then I found the bigger issue: **I never document anything in my life.**

## What Happens When You Don't Document

Here is what happens when you don't document anything:
- You forget the pain.
- You forget the mindset you had at that time.
- You forget the solution you found.
- You forget how you tackled the situation.

Now flip the side. Suppose you document everything. When you go back to that time, you know exactly what was happening. You know what you learned from that situation.

So I am planning to document my learning, my failures, my growth, my issues, and my solutions.

## The Real Reason I'm Writing This

But there is something else too.

We are living in an era where everybody has a voice. Everybody has a platform. Everybody has ideas. Everybody has a second brain.

And I am sitting in my room, completing every dream in my mind — while in reality, I am getting worse and worse day by day.

### How am I getting worse?

My goal used to be very big. And now it keeps getting smaller. I am adjusting with average things. I am settling.

So I planned: I will keep documenting myself. So that I know where I was. So that when I look back, I can see — **is there any improvement in my life, despite my age?**

## One More Thing

There is a hope in my mind. Some people in my community must know that there is a guy named **Aditya Ranjan**, who is also a developer.

I have started a new journey. I hope this journey teaches me something.
